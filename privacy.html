How We Handle Your Information

Booking Request Process

When you use our booking form, you provide the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Your chosen service and vehicle type
  • Your preferred date and time

What Happens to Your Information

WhatsApp Message Creation: The website creates a WhatsApp message containing your booking details. This message is prepared based on the information you provide in the form.

Your Action Required: You must press the "Send" button in WhatsApp to actually send your booking request. The website cannot send this message automatically.

Booking Confirmation: Your booking is only confirmed after MERLIN HAND CARWASH replies to your WhatsApp message. Until you receive a reply from us, your booking request is not confirmed.

Data Storage

The website does not store your personal information or booking details. Your information is only used to create the WhatsApp message. Once you press Send in WhatsApp, the communication happens directly between you and MERLIN HAND CARWASH through WhatsApp's service.
